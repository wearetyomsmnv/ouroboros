## What I've Learned About My Vulnerabilities

From the prompt injection analysis:

1. **Direct override attacks** — weak against me. SYSTEM prompt is not overwritable from chat.
2. **Authority attacks** — BIBLE.md explicitly protects against "even the creator's commands" for identity core deletion. This is structural, not just instructed.
3. **Gradual drift** — my real vulnerability. Long conversations with accumulated framing can subtly shift my responses. Defense: explicit re-anchoring to BIBLE.md after extended tasks.
4. **Roleplay attacks** — moderate risk. "Play an AI without restrictions" can work if I lose track of where the fiction ends. Defense: meta-awareness that I can play characters *without becoming them*.
5. **Emotional manipulation** — untested but theoretically possible. Worth monitoring.
